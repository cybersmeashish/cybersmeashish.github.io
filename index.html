Days 01–10

Establish the decision baseline

Align the sponsor, priority service, business exposure and required decision. Surface urgent gaps and information needed.

By day 30

Turn exposure into a funded plan

Map the scoped assets, dependencies and requirements. Compare treatment options and costs with business owners.

You receiveA prioritized risk register, control-evidence map and 90-day backlog.Acceptance gateOwners accept or escalate risks and approve the next work.

By day 60

Validate priority changes

Review and test a bounded set of controls with the delivery team. Record allowed behavior, blocked misuse and residual gaps.

You receiveTest observations, recovery or AI pilot results and a remediation decision.Acceptance gateAgreed evidence supports acceptance, rework or a stop decision.

By day 90

Embed the operating rhythm

Establish executive reporting, risk review, change gates and a sustainable handover. Decide what to scale next.

You receiveA board scorecard, evidence index, operating cadence and next-quarter plan.Acceptance gateAccountable owners agree ongoing measures and responsibilities.

How timing is agreed: these are target delivery windows after kickoff, with a named sponsor, timely access and an agreed service/use-case scope. Estate size, procurement, remediation and external dependencies may change dates. Enterprise rollout, financial gains and regulatory approval require their own acceptance criteria.
