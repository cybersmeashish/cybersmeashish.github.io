# Engagement method: business decisions supported by evidence

## Start with an agreed scope

Define the sponsor, priority business service or AI use case, required decision, affected people, systems, suppliers, jurisdictions and delivery team. Agree authorized review/test boundaries, confidentiality, responsibilities, fees and change control.

Proposed delivery targets assume timely access, available decision owners and an agreed bounded scope. Treat them as milestones for the engagement, not guarantees of estate-wide remediation, incident resolution, financial returns or regulatory approval.

| Target milestone | Leadership receives | Acceptance decision |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–10 | Business exposure brief, accountable risk owners and evidence request | Sponsor accepts scope, priorities and assumptions |
| By day 30 | Prioritized risk register, control-evidence map, treatment options and backlog | Owners accept, fund or escalate the next work |
| By day 60 | Bounded control/pilot/recovery observations and residual gaps | Accept, rework or stop against agreed evidence |
| By day 90 | Board scorecard, evidence index, operating rhythm and next-quarter plan | Assign ongoing ownership and agree the next investment |

## Make a result reviewable

Record the baseline, intended change, metric, target, owner, test/observation method, date, evidence, residual risk and acceptance decision. Test legitimate behavior alongside misuse cases. Count unique tests once even when repeated across environments.

- Recovery: record service scope, elapsed restore time, actual missing data, failed attempts and remaining dependencies.
- Access: record permitted and denied actions, relevant identity/tenant boundaries and remediation retests.
- AI: record task-specific evaluation, retrieval/tool permissions, human decision responsibility, change triggers and known failure cases.
- Compliance: identify the entity, jurisdiction, authoritative source/version, requirement, accountable owner, control and current evidence. Legal applicability and external attestations are separate determinations.
- Investment: distinguish measured results from assumptions, forecasts and scenario estimates. Compare feasible alternatives and recurring operating cost.

## Ongoing fractional leadership

Agree an operating cadence suited to the business: delivery-team review, monthly risk and investment decisions, executive reporting, supplier exceptions, incident exercises and periodic board discussion. Each report should show changed exposure, overdue decisions, evidence freshness and the next action.

## Publish a client case only when substantiated

Keep a permission and attribution record: organization, direct/employer-led/partner-led role, dates, authorized scope, confidentiality restrictions, baseline, measured result, measurement period, contribution and remaining limitations. Use named organizations only with permission to identify the engagement. Otherwise publish an appropriately anonymized, still substantiated case.

The current website links reference research and synthetic experiments. It does not present those experiments as client engagements.
